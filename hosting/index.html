Why Bani

Our family history lives in places that are easy to lose.

The elders' memory

Only a few people remember who descends from whom. When they pass, the stories go with them.

Photocopied charts

A chart gets printed once and never updated. New births and marriages go unrecorded.

WhatsApp groups

Addresses, phone numbers, and condolence news sink under hundreds of messages. Hard to find again.

Wall-sized charts

Horizontal trees need constant scrolling and zooming on a phone. Past four generations, they are unreadable.

Family tree

One tree, three ways to read it.

Pick what fits: follow a line of descent, look at one person with their parents and children, or see the whole chart.

Lineage: grows downward

Read the line of descent top to bottom, like a list. No sideways scrolling, however many generations deep.

Invite by branch

Every branch fills in its own family.

Send an invite over WhatsApp with an 8-letter code. Choose Contributor or View only, and set how many generations down they can edit.

Family events

Reunions, halal bihalal, and haul without manual tallies.

Relatives answer Going, Maybe, or Not going, plus how many are coming. Organizers see attendance per branch and dues per household.

Memory album

Ancestors' faces, kept on their profiles.

Photos, birth and death dates, spouse, occupation, and address on a map. Birthdays and haul memorials show up as reminders automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Can people outside our family see our tree?+

No. A tree is open only to people you invite. Phone numbers and addresses are stored separately and shown only to members of the tree.

Our elders don’t use smartphones. What then?+

No problem. Members don’t need an account. Their children or grandchildren can add their details and photos as branch contributors.

What’s the difference between Contributor and View only?+

Contributors can add and edit members in their branch, down to the generation limit you set. View only can open the tree, profiles, and events without changing anything.

Our data is already in Excel. Can we use it?+

Excel export is available for backups. Excel import is in the works; until then, our team can help move your data.

How do I pay for a plan?+

For now, plans are activated through the admin’s WhatsApp from the Profile menu. Paying directly through Google Play is coming soon.

Is there an iPhone version?+

The iOS version is in progress. For now, Bani is available on Android.
